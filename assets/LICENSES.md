## HDR panoramas (`assets/hdri/`): Poly Haven, CC0 1.0
Photographed by the Poly Haven team and contributors (https://polyhaven.com), public domain (CC0). They were obtained via the three.js examples (`mrdoob/three.js`, MIT) and `pmndrs/drei-assets`.
`spruit_sunrise` (1k, 4k), `venice_sunset`, `blouberg_sunrise_2`, `quarry_01`, `san_giuseppe_bridge`, `ferndale_studio_04`, `monochrome_studio_02`, `moonless_golf`, `royal_esplanade`, `pedestrian_overpass`, `empty_warehouse_01`, `studio_small_03`, `st_fagans_interior`, `lebombo`, `immenstadter_horn`, `kiara_1_dawn`, `potsdamer_platz`, `dikhololo_night`.
